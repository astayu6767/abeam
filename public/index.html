Questions, answered

No mystery in the machine.

Are there limits on targets I can hit?

No. Every plan comes with unlimited targets — Scout, Hunter and Bracket King all include it. Pay for slots, not per-name.

Is there a free tier?

No. abeam is a fully-managed paid service — a bot slot only starts once you have an active plan. That keeps every account honest.

Do I need to run anything on my PC?

No. We host the bots. You just make the slots online from the dashboard and watch the consoles.

What do I need for Minecraft?

An Xbox-authenticated Minecraft account. You paste its access token into a bot and the client signs in for you.

How do I pay?

Lightning-free, private LTC. Every invoice gets a fresh receive address so your wallet stays separate.
